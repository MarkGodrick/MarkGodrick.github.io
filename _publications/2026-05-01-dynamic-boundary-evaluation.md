This work has been **accepted to NeurIPS 2026 as a Poster**. We study **dynamic boundary evaluation** for language models: a framework for probing what models can and cannot do beyond fixed benchmarks and static worst-case attacks. The work formulates evaluation as an adaptive process that searches along model capability boundaries, guided by skills and calibrated by task difficulty.

The corresponding Chinese thesis, **大语言模型动态能力边界评测：技能引导搜索与难度校准**, was selected as an **Outstanding Undergraduate Thesis**.
